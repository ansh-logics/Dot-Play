Your music, on tape. 📼

title gets handwritten, and the reels actually wind as the song plays. Skip ahead and it fast-winds.

Piano keys that latch. A volume fader. A tape counter. Lyrics glowing on an amber screen.

And now your phone is the remote 📱
Scan one QR code and control the music from the couch, the kitchen, or anywhere. Any Wi-Fi, even mobile data.
